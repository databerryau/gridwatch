Tonight's desk

You decide which power stations run, and when. The dispatch system sets how hard each running unit works; you start more before the city needs them, and stop them when it does not. A coal unit takes two hours to reach the grid, a gas turbine ten minutes.

The line above the desk says what is needed next. To start a unit, click its ○ button on the lever bank (hydro units: beside the HYDRO wheel); it turns into START?, so click it again to confirm. Keys: 1–5 pick a lever (6 the HYDRO wheel), then S S. The battery's outer ring is its GUARD: MW held back to catch a trip. Space pauses at any time, and you can act while paused; the clock is held until you first press it. ? (top right) shows what GRIDWATCH simplifies.
