## Before: what you say, and nothing more

Send only the link: **https://databerryau.github.io/gridwatch/next.html**
Say: *"This is a game I'm making. Play today's day and say out loud what you're thinking."*
Their own computer, sound on. Then stay silent.

## The help rule

- Say nothing while they play. That includes no hints, no "nearly", and no pointing.
- **Help only after 60 s stuck.** Stuck means no useful action and no progress in what they say aloud. Time it.
- When you do help, give the smallest hint that gets them moving, and log it below.
