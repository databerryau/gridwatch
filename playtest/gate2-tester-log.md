## Before: what you say, and nothing more

Say: *"This is a game I'm making. I'll send it to you now. Play today's day and say out loud what you're thinking."*
Send your card (`gate2-invite.md`). Start the stopwatch when they open your message. Then stay silent.

## The rules

- The only prompt is *"keep talking"*.
- **Help only after 60 s stuck.** Stuck means no useful action and no progress in what they say aloud. Time it. Give the
  smallest hint that gets them moving, and log it below.
- **Never mention** sharing, the card, tomorrow, streaks or "the moment" (`gate2-README.md`, "What never to say").
- **After the verdict, stay silent** for at least 2 minutes, or until they say they're done.

## The app check

Ask: *"Could you scroll up to my message and send me a screenshot of it, as it arrived?"* If they tapped the link on
their phone: *"And one of the page it opened?"* Keep the screenshots off the repo.

**The preview rule:** the preview shows the picture and the title, and the description too where the app shows
descriptions. An app that never shows a description (iMessage puts the address there) passes on the picture and the
title. An app that previews no links at all (some SMS, most email), or none inside a longer message (R-2's step 3),
makes the preview n/a, and check 4 is then scored on the card and the phone page alone.
