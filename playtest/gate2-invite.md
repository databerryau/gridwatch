# GRIDWATCH gate 2: messages

Short, and the same for every tester. Copy them as they are; fill in the [brackets].

## 1. Booking (a day or two before)

No link and nothing about the game: the invitation has to arrive the way a stranger's would.

**SMS, WhatsApp, Messenger:**
> Have you got 20 minutes at your computer on [day] at [time]? I'd like to watch you try something I've made. Sound on if you can.

**Slack or Teams:**
> Got 20 minutes at your computer on [day] at [time]? I'd like to watch you try something I've made. Sound on if you can.

**Email.** Subject: *20 minutes on [day]?*
> Hi [name], have you got 20 minutes at your computer on [day] at [time]? I'd like to watch you try something I've made. Sound on if you can. I'll send it to you then.

Remote? Add: *"I'll call you on [app] and ask you to share your screen."*

## 2. The invitation (at the session)

**Your card, exactly as SHARE copied it, and nothing else.** That is how most cards arrive. If that feels too abrupt
for this person, one line above it: *"Your go."*

Don't add "beat that" or "send me yours": that asks them to share, and check 2 is about sharing unprompted.

What it looks like (yours will differ):

```
GRIDWATCH #5 · Fri 16 Oct · 975 A
█▅██▅▇█▁▅▆██  low 49.41 Hz
Lights  ★ every light on
Cost    +5% vs par
Carbon  ★ level with par
"Expensive lights. A scary dip."
databerryau.github.io/gridwatch
```

**By app:**
- **SMS, WhatsApp, Messenger, iMessage:** paste it as one message, nothing after the link. If the app builds a link
  preview while you type, wait for it before you press send.
- **Slack or Teams:** paste it as plain text, not in a code block (the grey box): the rows would line up, but the link stops
  being a link and makes no preview.
- **Email:** subject *GRIDWATCH #[N]*, the card as the body. Email shows no link preview, so check 4's preview can't be
  scored (the card's lines still can). Use it only for someone with no messaging app.

## 3. The day-2 check (two days after the session, in the morning)

Word for word, in the same app. Session on Monday: send it on Wednesday morning, asking about Tuesday.
> Thanks again for [session day]. One question for my notes, and no is a perfectly good answer: did you play GRIDWATCH on [the next day]?

If yes:
> Nice. What did you get, and what made you open it?

Then, either way:
> Thanks, that's all I needed. Play whenever you like from here.

Until this message is sent: no card of yours, no "did you see today's?", in any chat they are in.
